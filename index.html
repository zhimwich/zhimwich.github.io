Welcome to my website! I am a postdoc at UC Berkeley, sponsored by Shirshendu Ganguly. Previously, I obtained my PhD in 2025 at Columbia University, where I was advised by Ivan Corwin. I am interested in a variety of topics in probability, combinatorics, and mathematical physics, mostly related to the KPZ equation and associated integrable models.
